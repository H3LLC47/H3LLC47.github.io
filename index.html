01 / about

About

I work for a university system made up of several semi-autonomous institutions, as the go-to expert for its collaboration, endpoint, and network management platforms. Each campus runs at a different level of technical maturity, and a big part of my job is bringing them onto a consistent, manageable foundation.

I got here by working through nearly every layer of IT: MSP help desk, field engineering, IT management, systems engineering, and systems administration. That background means I design networks with the servers, identity systems, and support staff on the other end of them in mind.
